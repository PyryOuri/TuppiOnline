The frozen `Certificate` carries source information key, root seat/action,
objective, control model, complete-support digest, immutable information-key/action
entries, terminal upper bound, and completeness/generation-budget provenance.
`verify_certificate(observation, certificate, budget)` rebuilds the complete
N02 support independently of the asserted count and compares its allocation
contents digest. Neither a one-world digest nor a forged full count passes.

Two explicit control models avoid ambiguity:

- `SINGLE_SEAT`: follow the supplied plan at the controlled seat; enumerate all
  legal choices at all other seats, including the partner. A sole legal own
  action can be implicit. Missing non-forced decisions, illegal choices,
  conflicting entries, root conflicts and unreachable/non-observation keys fail.
- `ALL_ACTIONS`: after the specified root play, enumerate arbitrary legal play
  at every seat, including the root. A plan cannot constrain this model.

`all_action_bounds(observation, root_action, budget)` uses the latter model and
rebuilds support itself. Universal verification invokes this comparator for the
baseline using the **remaining** operation budget. A universal claim requires
the exhaustively verified candidate upper bound to be at most the baseline's
all-actions lower bound, plus a feasible strict case. Verifying an upper bound
alone yields `UNCLASSIFIED`; N03 does not claim to solve the N04 security problem.

Results preserve sets of correlated `(own tricks, final score state)` outcomes,
with separate totals/score-set views. Path counts are diagnostics, **not
probabilities**. Research paths retain a hypothetical assignment and legal action
sequence. They are excluded from result repr and public diagnostics. Terminal
counterexamples are replayed through the ordinary engine in the tests; missing
or illegal own decisions retain the legal prefix reaching the failed key.
Structural input/digest/conflict failures have explicit reasons rather than
invented terminal counterexamples.

## Concrete counterexamples

These are research-private synthetic layouts at the supplied decision point.
Play notation is `seat:card`; the supplied fixture contains the preceding history.

**B, wrong continuation.** Remaining hands by seat:
`0={9C,3H,3C}`, `1={2C,7C}`, `2={5H,3S}`, `3={4C,10C}`.

```text
0:9C  0:3H  1:2C  2:5H  3:4C  2:3S  3:10C  0:3C  1:7C
```

The legal path ends at six own tricks and score `4–0`, disproving the claimed
at-most-four bound. It is one retained counterexample, not a new selected deal.

**C, false universal comparison.** Both paths use the same remaining hands:
`0={3D,2S}`, `1={4C,10C}`, `2={KC,2D,10D}`, `3={JC,QC}`.

```text
10D: 2:10D 3:JC 0:3D 1:4C 2:KC 2:2D 3:QC 0:2S 1:10C  -> 6, score 4–0
 KC: 2:KC  3:JC 0:3D 1:4C 2:2D 3:QC 0:2S 1:10C 2:10D -> 4, score 12–0
```

Both continuations are engine-legal. This refutes the requested universal
comparison; it is not a claim about expected play or a computed equilibrium.
