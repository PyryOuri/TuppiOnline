## What was built

[`src/tuppi_nolo/security.py`](../../../src/tuppi_nolo/security.py), entry point
`solve_security(observation, budget, *, verification_budget=None)`. It returns
every root action's security value, one plan for the selected action, a
certificate, and that certificate's independent verdict from N03 — or a typed
unresolved status carrying no value.

The recursion is the one the task fixes:

```
terminal:    V(S) = max own-team final tricks over the states in S
root's turn: V(S) = min over actions legal in *every* state of S
other turn:  V(S) = max over the union of legal actions, each filtered to
             exactly the states where it was legal
```

The controlled seat commits to one action per information key; every other seat,
**partner included**, is quantified over arbitrary legal actions. `solve_late_game`
is not used anywhere: it sees the hidden hands and assumes a cooperating partner,
which is a different objective. Ties are settled by the inherited v2 ranking
rather than iteration order, and own nodes are partitioned by semantic key before
any choice is made.

## Why per-world minimisation would be invalid — and an honest negative

The abstract control is the tiny two-world reference: worlds share one future
observation, action A rewards X in world 0 and Y in world 1, action B pays 0.65
in both. Choosing per world claims **1.0** and is not implementable. With one
shared choice the same position is worth **0.5 in expectation** and **0.0 in the
worst case**; both objectives select B, and the two numbers are asserted to
differ so they cannot be quietly conflated.

**On A, B and C, per-world minimisation happens to agree with the
information-legal value at every root:**

| Fixture | Clairvoyant per-world worst | Information-legal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | `8S`=7, `QD`=9, `3H`=9 | identical |
| B | `9C`=4, `3C`=5 | identical |
| C | all 6 | identical |

That agreement is a property of these three positions, not evidence that the
shortcut is sound. Inspecting B shows why: after the first own follow-up the two
worlds produce *different* information keys, so there is nothing to fuse. These
fixtures simply do not exercise strategy fusion at the root, which is precisely
why the task required a constructed counterexample rather than accepting a
fixture as the demonstration. A future position with genuinely indistinguishable
continuations would separate the two, and the recursion here is already written
for that case.

## Independent check and negative controls

A brute-force reference in the test file enumerates **complete** own plans and
only then lets the opposition answer — generate-and-test rather than recursive
minimisation. It agrees with the module on every root of B, with zero value
differences. An earlier version of that reference grouped *partial* plans and
consequently reported 3 where the true value is 5; the failure is recorded here
because it is exactly the mistake the reference exists to catch.

Checked and rejected as required: a legal-but-wrong action substituted at a real
choice, a bound claimed one trick better than achieved (`DISPROVED_BOUND`),
a dropped branch at a node where the seat genuinely had alternatives
(`ILLEGAL_PLAN`, and note that dropping a *forced* entry is correctly harmless),
zero budget, and a non-acting observer (`INVALID_INPUT`). Invariance holds under
swapping the hidden layout behind one observation and under every permutation of
the support order. Diagnostics carry no assignment, hand or private path.

## Limitations

The partner is adversarial in this model. That is deliberate and conservative,
and it will understate what a cooperating partnership can achieve — the value
reported is protection one seat can guarantee alone, not the team optimum and
not an equilibrium. Where that is too pessimistic the remedy is a stated control
model, not a silently omniscient partner.

Only the three supplied positions were measured. Nothing here shows the objective
helps in play; that is N07's question and, beyond it, N09's.
