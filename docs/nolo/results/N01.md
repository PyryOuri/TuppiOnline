## Method and boundary

The research-only [loader](../../../src/tuppi_nolo/fixtures.py) validates the task
schema, dealer, full deck, four 13-card hands, score and prefix grouping, then
replays forced canonical NOLO shows and every play through the ordinary engine.
It preserves initial hand order. Expected answers never enter the replay object.
Private fixtures and `DealState` remain in the harness; the baseline receives
only `PlayerObservation`. No policy, profile, production integration or optimized
support/search/verifier was added.

The [independent helper](../../../tests/nolo/evidence_reference.py) ports the
framing appendix: derive remaining capacities from public plays, enumerate every
unconstrained partition, and replay the entire public history using
[`hypothetical_continuation`](../../../src/tuppi_exact/continuation.py).
A separate card-by-card recursion enumerates
[`HardConstraintModel`](../../../src/tuppi_exact/constraints.py), using its known
holdings, capacities and allowed seats. The tests compare the **full assignment
sets**, ignoring only card order within allocations, and compare their sizes
with [`feasible_assignment_count`](../../../src/tuppi_exact/counting.py).
The observer's actual hand and legal-action order are also checked across worlds.

All-path traversal calls the ordinary engine on every legal action, without
sampling, caching, equivalent-card pruning or a continuation policy. C additionally
uses independent engine-based cooperative minimax at every world and root card,
cross-checked against [`solve_late_game`](../../../src/tuppi_exact/solver.py).
That minimax grants full information and cooperative partnership control; it is
not the single-seat imperfect-information security solver scheduled for N04.

## Three input corrections

1. **An avoidable win does not imply absence of a lower card.** In
   [witness B](../framings/1.md#witness-b-an-avoidable-win-can-be-the-correct-first-move),
   seat 0 holds `9C 3H 3C`, can legally duck with `3C`, yet taking `9C` and then
   leading `3C` guarantees four. The lower card remains in its hand immediately
   after the win. Follow-suit legality in
   [`trick_play.py`](../../../src/tuppi_rules/trick_play.py) does not mandate
   ducking. Inferring no lower card requires an additional behavioural model.
2. **Losing margins can matter.**
   [`score_nolo`](../../../src/tuppi_rules/scoring.py) gives the opponent eight
   points for `(8,5)` and twelve for `(9,4)`. At `0–40`,
   [`transition_rising_score`](../../../src/tuppi_rules/match.py) yields `0–48`
   versus terminal `0–52`. Witness A similarly distinguishes seven from nine
   own tricks despite the deal already being lost.
3. **Only down-team winning margins are always erased.** A down-team win resets
   to `0–0` at every winning split; a down-team loss adds to the opponent's rise.
   Equivalently, an up-team loss resets regardless of margin. Up-team wins add
   points, while overshoot past 52 adds no match-winning value. The transition
   and [`match_transition_value`](../../../src/tuppi_rules/match.py) implement
   these distinctions; the latter is a score-state proxy, not match equity.

The charter, game object and historical framing were not edited.
