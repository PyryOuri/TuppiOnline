## Implementation and scope

Added [support.py](../../../src/tuppi_nolo/support.py) and
[test_support.py](../../../tests/nolo/test_support.py). No earlier package,
policy, profile, fixture, historical document or N01 helper was changed.

`build_support(PlayerObservation, SupportBudget)` requires resolved NOLO and at
most three remaining tricks. It checks public consistency, builds the existing
hard model, and counts **before** enumerating. Cards are visited by number of
allowed owners, then canonical deck index; seats ascend. Known holdings are
never reassigned. The observing hand retains its order; other hands are canonical.
Every assignment is replayed by the existing `hypothetical_continuation`.

`SupportResult` distinguishes `EXACT_COMPLETE`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `SUPPORT_BUDGET`,
`SEARCH_BUDGET`, and `INVALID_INPUT`. Wrong entry types raise `TypeError`.
Contradictory evidence returns `INVALID_INPUT`; a rejected enumerated candidate,
duplicate, count disagreement or changed semantic replay raises
`SupportInvariantError` (`FAILED_CHECK`), blocking downstream certification.
Unexpected programming errors propagate. No rejected candidate is silently
removed from a purported exact support.

Budget exhaustion returns no worlds and `world_count=None`, even if some
replays already completed. `counted_candidates` describes only the hard-model
count used for admission. It does not promote an incomplete replay to exactness.

Private assignments and hypothetical states are confined to `research_worlds`
on the research result and excluded from its repr and `diagnostics()` output.
Diagnostics carry no cards, owner maps, weights, sample identities or protocol
references. No policy receives this result. Its `UNCLASSIFIED` label makes no
action, security or universal-improvement claim; those operations await later tasks.

The semantic observation key follows the public-field design in
[`information_state.py`](../../../src/tuppi_search/information_state.py), without
importing or editing that package. It includes own-hand/legal-action order,
showing and trick history, seat/partnership, score, actor, phase and rules/schema
identity. Protocol match/deal references are absent; sample indices and hidden
allocations never enter it. World content keys are separate, research-private
canonical allocations. There are no optional weights or behavioural exclusions.
