# Phase 5G-B results

The executable report is generated by
`tools/run_phase5g_b_evaluation.py` and is written to the external
`TuppiRuns/phase5g-b` directory. It does not serialize synthetic hidden-hand
labels. The committed report below is a compact record of the protocol and the
decision; bulk position-level output remains outside Git.

## Protocol

The belief prior is the Phase 5B exact hard-feasible uniform sampler. The fixed
behavior model uses epsilon `0.10`; the robust mixture uses weights `(1/3, 1/3,
1/3)` for reactive, public-counting, and partner-aware frozen policies. Showing
and trick actions are scored causally, while the root observer's own prior
actions are excluded. Search integration is false.

Synthetic games are homogeneous known-policy games from the three frozen Phase 4
profiles. Development, calibration, and held-out streams have disjoint roots.
The held-out report contains matched-policy, misspecified-reactive, robust
mixture, and hard-uniform metrics, plus ESS, reliability, ablations, candidate
count convergence, and independent tiny exact validation.

The candidate-32 held-out run used 56 development, 57 calibration, and 57 held-out
positions (three generating
profiles and disjoint stream roots). On held-out positions, hard-uniform owner
NLL was `1.050`, matched smoothed was `1.747`, and the equal mixture was `1.758`.
The corresponding mean ESS for weighted candidates was `6.47/32`; candidate
evaluation throughput was approximately 27 matched or 25 mixture candidates per
second, with median per-position latencies of `0.85 s` and `0.97 s`.

The independent tiny exact reference gave a matched-policy NLL improvement of
`0.314` over exact uniform (six positions; mean approximate-versus-exact owner
marginal TV `0.043`). Candidate-prefix ESS increased from `6.9` at 16 to
`44.3` at 128 on the diagnostic corpus, but adjacent marginal TV remained
non-negligible (`0.093` for 32-to-64 and `0.189` for 64-to-128 in this small
sample). These are finite-particle results, not claims about the exact posterior
on the full game state.

## Interpretation rule

The phase gate is deliberately conservative:

- **B1**: matched and robust beliefs improve held-out owner metrics with workable
  ESS and runtime;
- **B2**: the matched oracle helps but the generic mixture is fragile;
- **B3**: matched weighting adds little information over hard uniform;
- **B4**: the signal or its finite-particle computation is not yet practical.

Synthetic matched-policy success would not establish human-belief accuracy. Any
future search integration belongs to Phase 5G-C and must consume a versioned
weighted-particle interface without changing this causal/privacy boundary.

## Phase gate

The selected outcome is **B4 — belief signal works in tiny exact spaces, but the
finite weighted candidate representation is currently too concentrated for
reliable held-out use at a practical budget**. No search agent consumes these
beliefs. The next phase should first address candidate degeneracy or a compact
likelihood/belief approximation; it must not silently substitute this finite
posterior into PIMC, UCT, or ISMCTS.
